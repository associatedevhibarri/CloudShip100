Paste these into New Booking or the home quote form. Write addresses as street, city, postal code, country.

Always get a new quote before you book. Uber prices only last about 15 minutes.

Economy, Standard, Premium, and On-demand are filters you use after prices come back. They are not fields on the form.

After each quote

Use Partner to show only Courier Guy, Uber Direct, or FedEx.
Use Speed to show Economy, Standard, Premium, or On-demand.
Use Sort for lowest price or fastest.

One search will not return all three. Sandton to Cape Town is Courier Guy. Johannesburg local is Uber (Courier Guy might appear too). US addresses are FedEx.
